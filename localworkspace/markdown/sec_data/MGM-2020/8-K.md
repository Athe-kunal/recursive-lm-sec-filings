Item 5.02    Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers

(b) On December 4, 2020, MGM Resorts International (the “Company”) announced that Atif Rafiq has stepped down as the Company’s President—Commercial & Growth, effective December 4, 2020. Mr. Rafiq will remain an employee of the Company through December 18, 2020.

(c) On December 4, 2020, the Company announced that Corey Sanders has been appointed Chief Operating Officer, effective December 4, 2020. Mr. Sanders will continue to serve as Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer until a replacement is hired and is in place. Mr. Sanders, age 57, has served as Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer of the Company since March 2019 and previously as Chief Operating Officer of the Company from 2010 to March 2019. Mr. Sanders has no family relationships with any of our directors or executive officers and there are no transactions in which Mr. Sanders has an interest in that would require disclosure under Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K. The employment agreement with Mr. Sanders, effective as of April 1, 2020, and filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 31, 2020, is incorporated herein by reference.
